# 🎉 SeasonalBots

Welcome to **SeasonalBots** — a collection of seasonal Discord bots and services designed to bring interactive events, games, countdowns, and festive experiences to Discord communities throughout the year.

SeasonalBots is owned and operated by **NodeByte LTD**.

---

## 🌟 Our Bots

Each SeasonalBot is designed around a specific time of year, with its own identity, features, events, and community experiences.

| Bot                 | Theme               | Key Features                                                                          | Status            |
| ------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| **HalloweenBot** 🎃 | Spooky & mysterious | Seasonal events, interactive games, Halloween countdowns, server customisation & more | ✅ Active          |
| **ChristmasBot** 🎄 | Festive & cheerful  | Christmas events, festive games, countdowns & seasonal server features                | ✅ Active          |
| **EasterBot** 🐣    | Bright & playful    | Easter events, egg hunts, spring countdowns & interactive activities                  | 🚧 In Development |

## 🚀 The SeasonalBots Platform

SeasonalBots is more than just a collection of Discord bots. Our central platform brings our services together under one ecosystem.

The SeasonalBots platform includes:

* 🔗 **Linked Services** — Connect your HalloweenBot, ChristmasBot, and future SeasonalBots accounts.
* 🖥️ **Central Dashboard** — Manage your connected services and servers from one place.
* 💎 **SeasonalBots Premium** — Manage Premium subscriptions and assign Premium benefits to supported servers.
* 🎫 **Support Platform** — Create and manage support tickets directly through SeasonalBots.
* 📊 **Status Platform** — Monitor the availability and health of SeasonalBots services.
* 📚 **Documentation** — Guides and resources for using our bots and services.
* 📰 **Blog & Changelog** — Keep up with new features, improvements, and announcements.
* 🧪 **Beta Programme** — Help test upcoming SeasonalBots features before public release.

---

## 💎 SeasonalBots Premium

SeasonalBots Premium provides additional features and customisation across supported SeasonalBots services.

Premium subscriptions are managed centrally through SeasonalBots, allowing eligible Premium entitlements to be assigned to supported Discord servers and synchronised with services such as HalloweenBot.

Premium features may include:

* Custom embeds and branding
* Advanced countdown configuration
* Webhook support
* Additional custom commands
* Welcome and farewell customisation
* Advanced moderation features
* Analytics
* Beta feature access
* Priority support

Available features may vary depending on the bot, Premium plan, and service.

## ⚙️ Technology

Our services use a modern stack designed for scalability, reliability, and maintainability.

* 🧠 **Runtime:** Node.js
* 🤖 **Discord:** Discord.js
* 🗄️ **Database:** PostgreSQL
* 🔷 **ORM:** Prisma
* ⚡ **APIs:** Fastify & Next.js API Routes
* 🖥️ **Web Applications:** Next.js & React
* 🎨 **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
* 🔐 **Authentication:** Discord OAuth
* 💳 **Payments:** Stripe
* 🐳 **Deployment:** Docker

Individual repositories may use different technologies depending on their requirements.
